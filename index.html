TT Alarm Bot

Screenshot a table tennis match. Get an alarm before it starts.

  • Reads the players, start time, over/under and edge straight from your screenshots.
  • Rings in your browser a few minutes before each match.
  • Sign in to keep your bets, background and settings on every device.

Without an account, everything is saved on this device only.

Alarms
Alarms

Turn alarms off to use the app without them: matches are still listed and still get Cash / Chalk once they start. On phones, the ring and the notification each have their own switch, so you can keep just the notification, for example. The ring stops after 10 seconds, and opening the app stops the alarm. Phones: an alarm only rings while the app is open on screen. If the phone locks or you switch apps, the phone pauses the app until you come back, so keep the screen on (the switch above does this while a bet is coming up). Test alarm rings in 5 seconds so you can check the sound and notification. These alarm settings are for this device only: your other phones and computers keep their own.

used when you press Cash

With the switch on and average odds set, Cash records the win straight away at these odds with your last-used units, just like Chalk. Turn it off (or leave the odds empty) to type the odds each time. Use + for several bets, or click the entry in History to change it.

filled in when you press Cash

The Cash popup (and each new slot for several bets) starts with these odds already filled in. You can change them before saving; nothing is recorded until you press Save. Leave empty to type them each time.

units for bets tagged Bot

With this on, bets tagged Bot start with these units (Cash / Chalk buttons and the Cash popup) instead of your last-used units, and recording them doesn't change your last-used units.

Tags

Tags show next to OVER/UNDER on your alarms. Click an alarm to add or remove its tags.

League Links

Switch a league off to hide its bets: yours (they won't ring either), friends' bets and bell messages. P/L and History still include every league. Switch it back on and they come back. Click the player names on a bet to open its league's link; clear a link to go back to its default.
